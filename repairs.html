Hardware Upgrades & Repair Log

Crashed HDD replaced with an SSD

Problem: The computer failed to boot into the operating system and displayed a "No Bootable Device" error message along with a faint clicking sound from the drive.

What I did: I unplugged the power, opened the case, and disconnected the SATA data and power cables from the failed hard drive. I mounted a new SSD into the drive bay, reconnected the cables, and performed a clean operating system installation using a bootable USB flash drive.

Result: The computer booted up normally without errors and run quietly, and reliably.

Slow laptop upgraded from HDD → SSD

Problem: The laptop took over two minutes to start up and froze frequently when opening basic programs or web browsers.

What I did: I connected a new SSD to the laptop using a USB-to-SATA adapter and cloned the existing hard drive onto it. Next, I removed the bottom panel of the laptop, disconnected the battery, swapped out the old mechanical drive for the cloned SSD, and reassembled the casing.

Result: Startup time dropped to under 15 seconds, and applications opened almost instantly.

Phone microphone sub-board replaced

Problem: People could not hear my voice during phone calls, and voice memos recorded only static unless I used speakerphone or headphones.

What I did: I powered off the phone, gently heated the back cover to soften the adhesive, and pried it open with a plastic pick. After unscrewing the lower shield and disconnecting the battery flex cable, I removed the faulty charging and microphone sub-board, installed the replacement board, reconnected the cables, and sealed the back cover.

Result: The primary microphone worked clearly again on regular calls and audio recordings.
